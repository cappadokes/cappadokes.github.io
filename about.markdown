I'm Christos Lamprakos, and texts are my vice. I grew up in a small village next to the [Ancient Theatre of Epidaurus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Theatre_of_Epidaurus) and hold a PhD in Computer Engineering from [NTU Athens](https://ntua.gr/en/) and [KU Leuven](https://www.kuleuven.be/english/kuleuven/index.html).

Christaras is a Latin version of the Greek word Χρηστάρας, which in turn is an exaggerated version of the Greek name [Χρήστος](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%CE%A7%CF%81%CE%AE%CF%83%CF%84%CE%BF%CF%82) (Christos--c'est moi). The root of the name is the Ancient Greek word [χρηστός](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%CF%87%CF%81%CE%B7%CF%83%CF%84%CF%8C%CF%82#Ancient_Greek), of which the most common meaning is "useful", but has also been used by authorities such as Aristophanes and Plato to denote the "fool", or the "idiot"--Greek speakers may find [this page](https://www.greek-language.gr/digitalResources/ancient_greek/tools/liddel-scott/search.html?lq=%CE%A7%CF%81%CE%B7%CF%83%CF%84%CF%8C%CF%82) interesting.
